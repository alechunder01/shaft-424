Full Lore Text (ready for your README or intro cutscene)
In the late 2080s, a devastating global war engulfed Earth. Nuclear and hydrogen bombs erased entire nations. Billions died. The survivors fled to orbital sky cities, lunar colonies, and makeshift habitats in deep space.
By the time the last missiles fell in the early 2090s, Earth was barely habitable. What followed was decades of economic collapse, famine, and political chaos. All off-world expansion programs were frozen as every remaining resource was funneled into survival and reconstruction.
Mars, which had seen its first permanent outposts in the 2060s and 2070s, was completely abandoned. Entire colonies went silent. For nearly fifty years, the red planet belonged only to dust and wind.

In 2118, as Earth slowly stabilized, a new race to claim Mars began. The Czech Republic, drawing on its proud industrial heritage from Ostrava and the legendary Vítkovice ironworks, returned to its greatest pre-war asset: Shaft 424 — the deepest and most successful geothermal borehole ever drilled on Mars.
Shaft 424 reached a massive underground lava lake, providing near-unlimited energy and access to the rare high-temperature mineral Vítkovium. The plan was ambitious: reactivate the shaft, restart mining operations, and lay the foundations for Nová Ostrava — the first permanent Czech colony on Mars.
A reactivation team of twenty specialists was sent in cryo-sleep. Upon arrival, they began assessing the condition of the old Vítkovice-built facility. Only hours after landing, a violent planet-wide dust storm (or something far more mysterious) forced the team to take shelter in the emergency habitat prepared by autonomous rovers years earlier.
With the surface becoming lethal, the crew entered cryo-sleep to wait out the long Martian winter.
All except one.
You, the chief geothermal engineer, never woke up with the others. Technical failure. The team, believing you had died in cryo, left a small bouquet of synthetic flowers and a final message on a tablet beside your chamber. They evacuated on the emergency return vehicle, carrying the news of your death back to Earth.

You have just awakened.
The year is 2143.
The base is running on emergency power. The great lava generators are offline. The return ship sits partially dismantled. Communication with Earth is impossible without restoring main power.
You are alone.
Fatigued, disoriented, and abandoned in the abandoned shaft CZ424.
Now you must explore the silent complex, restore power from the ancient lava core, uncover what really happened to your crew… and decide your future:

Repair the ship and escape back to Earth, or
Risk everything to stabilize the shaft and become the founder of New Vítkovice on Mars.

Two paths.
One abandoned shaft.
Your choice will echo across the red planet.
